## Objective

Prepare the first paid acquisition flight that invites a visitor to start a **seven-day Essentials trial** of LTS Inventory, with **no card required**, at **$49 per month after the trial**.

This document packages copy, assets, naming, tracking, and checklists so a human can build the campaigns **manually in a paused/draft state**. It does not create, enable, or fund ads.

## Destination

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Approved campaign destination | https://inventory.lit-solutions.tech |
| Display URL | inventory.lit-solutions.tech |
| Signup path (same host; use if the ad platform needs the trial-start URL) | https://inventory.lit-solutions.tech/api/auth/login?mode=signup |

Do not send this flight to `https://lit-solutions.tech` unless the owner later changes the approved destination.

## Offer (only this offer)

State all three parts whenever the format has room:

1. Seven-day Essentials trial
2. No card required
3. $49 per month after the trial

Do not advertise Operations ($149), Control ($399), Enterprise, annual prices, or the grandfathered $19.99 assignment in this flight.

## Audience (directional, not researched)

Operators and small inventory teams who currently rely on spreadsheets or clipboards and need lot, serial, receiving, and stock history they can review.

No customer counts, industry share, or “used by” claims. No invented persona research.

## Message

Lead with the public product line already on the marketing site:

- **Know what you have.**
- **Know where it went.**
- **Inventory you can explain.**

Support with Essentials-safe capabilities only: products, lots, serials, receiving, counts, returns, reports, and stock history.

## Creative policy

- Reuse v3 PNGs. Do not redraw assets that already fit size and offer.
- Hold any creative that shows a capability **not included in Essentials** (see asset manifest).
- Do not add testimonials, logos of other companies, compliance badges, or performance callouts.

## Success for this packaging work

- One brief, channel copy, limits, manifest, naming, UTMs, and checklists exist in-repo.
- A reviewer can paste copy and upload listed assets without inventing claims.
- First-flight instructions end in **paused / draft**. Spend requires a separate owner decision.

## Out of scope

- Launching, publishing, or attaching a payment method in any ad account
- Recreating v3 PNGs
- Changing the live product or marketing site
- Inventing features, savings, social proof, or results
- Conversion-pixel implementation on the app (noted as a gap, not built here)
