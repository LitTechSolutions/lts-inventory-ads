## Superseded v3 LinkedIn line

Do not paste: “Products, locations, lots, serials, purchasing, receiving, transfers, and history you can stand behind.”

Purchasing, transfers, and fulfillment are not listed as Essentials inclusions on the public plan comparison. “Stand behind” reads like an endorsement or guarantee this pack does not make.
